## 📈 Common Sales KPIs

- **Total Revenue:** Sum of revenue from eligible sales transactions.
- **Total Orders:** Number of distinct qualifying invoices.
- **Units Sold:** Total quantity sold under the defined sales rules.
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** Sales revenue divided by the number of qualifying orders.
- **Revenue Growth:** Percentage change in revenue between comparable periods.
- **Product Contribution:** Percentage of total revenue generated by a product.
- **Regional Contribution:** Percentage of total revenue generated by a geographic market.
- **Profit Margin:** Profit divided by revenue, where reliable cost and profit data are available.

KPI definitions, filters, and calculation rules will be documented within each project to maintain consistency.

## 🔍 Analytical Approach

Each project follows a structured workflow:

1. Define the business problem and objectives.
2. Identify the dataset and understand its structure.
3. Validate data quality and handle missing values appropriately.
4. Clean and prepare data for analysis.
5. Perform exploratory data analysis.
6. Calculate relevant business KPIs.
7. Analyze trends, comparisons, and performance drivers.
8. Create visualizations and dashboards.
9. Interpret findings and develop business recommendations.
10. Document the methodology, assumptions, and limitations.
